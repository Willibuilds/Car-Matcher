About MotorUnite

Car buying is broken.
We fixed it.

MotorUnite is a free AI-powered car recommendation tool built for UK drivers who are tired of endless comparison sites, pushy salespeople, and conflicting advice. We ask three questions. We give you one answer.

The Problem

The UK car market is overwhelming

There are thousands of models available in the UK at any given time. Comparison sites show you hundreds of results. Dealerships have their own agenda. Friends and family all have different opinions. And most people — especially first-time buyers — have no framework for deciding what actually matters to them.

The result? People spend weeks researching, visit multiple dealerships, feel pressured into decisions, and still aren't sure they bought the right car. It's one of the most stressful purchases most people will ever make — and it doesn't need to be.

"Tell us your budget, how you drive, and what fuel you prefer. We'll tell you exactly which car to buy."
How It Works

Three questions. One recommendation.
